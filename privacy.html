Privacy notice

Loyalty card · Last updated November 2026

Who we are

The Parallel is a coffee and toast bar at 143 Barrack St, Perth WA 6000. This notice explains what we do with the details you give us when you join our loyalty card.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and email.
  • Your birthday, only if you choose to give it.
  • Your stamps and rewards: when and at which counter they were given.
  • Whether you agreed to receive offers from us.

Why

  • To run your loyalty card: keep your stamps, recognise your card and give you your reward.
  • To send you offers and news by email or message, only if you ticked that box. You can say no at any time, and every message tells you how to stop them.
  • If you gave your birthday, to send you a birthday treat (only if you agreed to offers).

We don't sell your details or share them with other businesses for their marketing.

Where it is kept

Your card runs on Afrovai, the loyalty platform we use. Its data is stored on servers in Sydney, Australia. Only our team and the people who keep the platform running can see it, and only to do their job.

How long

We keep your details while you have a card. You can delete your card and your details at any time: open your card and tap "Delete my card", or ask us at the counter.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to delete them. If you're unhappy with how we handled your details, tell us first; you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Contact

Ask our team at the counter, or message us on Instagram at @theparallelinperth.
